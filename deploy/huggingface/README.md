# EcoRisk AI

Human activity weakens the land; weather pulls the trigger. EcoRisk AI predicts what happens next at a
site (how fragile the ground is, and whether an extreme-rainfall trigger is coming in the next 24-72
hours) and turns it into an impact estimate and an action plan for an operation.

Source, methodology, validation and data disclosure: https://github.com/kirthika604/EcoRisk
