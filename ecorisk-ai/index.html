      4 · Predict on unseen data Live input

      Give EcoRisk AI a weather record it has never seen: at least 31 consecutive days of date, rainfall, temperature, humidity, pressure and wind. Common column names (rainfall, temp, humidity, pressure in hPa or kPa) are mapped automatically, and missing dew point is derived. The result appears in the Site outlook above.

      A new location gets the rain forecast. A structural score needs the satellite land-change trends, which exist only for the study sites.

        6 · Stay safe: turn a prediction into action Follows your outlook

        A prediction only matters if someone acts on it. The level that matches the latest outlook above is highlighted. Always follow official warnings from IMD, NDMA and your district administration first.

        Normal Current outlook

        No flag from either model.

        • Know your nearest safe shelter and two ways to reach it.
        • Keep an emergency kit: water, torch, medicines, documents, power bank.
        • Save the helpline numbers below.

        Watch Current outlook

        Fragile land, or heavy rain likely, but not both.

        • Check IMD and local forecasts twice a day.
        • Clear drains around your home and keep valuables off the floor.
        • Avoid trekking, river banks and road cuts on steep slopes.

        Act Current outlook

        Fragile land and an extreme-rain alert together.

        • Be ready to move to higher, stable ground at short notice.
        • Do not cross flowing water or stay near steep slopes overnight.
        • Follow district evacuation orders immediately. Help elderly neighbours.

        Warning signs of a landslide

        • New cracks in the ground, walls or roads
        • Trees, poles or fences starting to tilt
        • Doors and windows suddenly jamming
        • Rumbling sounds, or falling rocks and soil

        Warning signs of a flash flood

        • Stream water suddenly turning muddy
        • River level rising fast, or carrying debris
        • Unusually heavy rain upstream, even if it is dry where you are
        • A roaring sound from the valley

        How human activity raises risk

        • Cutting trees removes roots that bind slope soil
        • Unplanned construction loads slopes and blocks drainage
        • Road cuts at the toe of a slope remove its support
        • Paving over land sends rain straight into streams
